# Spell card illustrations

All 35 Spell cards use distinct magical scenes in public/art/spell-portraits.webp. Stable SP-01 through SP-35 IDs map to seven columns and five rows, in row-major order. Card mechanics, learning costs, clickable Sigil sockets and the external activation panel are preserved.

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool and encoded locally as WebP for offline play, following the Companion and Item portrait atlas approach.
