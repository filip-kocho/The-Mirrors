Black Mirror curses resolve automatically or through validated choices; no arbitrary manual state-editing form is used. Default games cannot skip curses. An explicit optional playtest configuration permits skipping. Other unimplemented card families do not claim execution.

Latest interface updates: take arrows use the same capacity validation as transfers; Spell learning costs appear at the upper-left of the card, and leaving remains a board action. Learned slots show the catalog's Quick/Permanent Sigil requirements with used-Sigil indicators; activate by opening the card. The opening page uses paired Mirror artwork, automatic random setup without a seed field, a tabbed 155-card collection and a detailed How to Play guide. Kaleidoscope focus follows cell geometry without a square frame.

Companion figurines: pale card-specific miniatures follow their holder. v0.4 adds LOYAL and STRAY behavior, curse abandonment and room recruitment. Per-card behavior assignments default to LOYAL pending designer mapping; Rex/Coco/Mamba and Alexis/Julius conflicts now use configurable review defaults; other printed abilities remain NOT IMPLEMENTED.
