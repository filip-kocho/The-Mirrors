Black Mirror v0.4 is implemented. All provisional interpretations A-N and migration policies are listed in BLACK_MIRROR_IMPLEMENTATION.md. Spell casting remains a separate unfinished module; Sigil Seal state and suppression predicates are ready for its integration.

Next implementation priorities: Spell casting/Quick refresh, remaining Companion abilities, tiny scenario configuration and expanded developer tooling.

Companion behavior now uses only LOYAL/STRAY. No resident/wanderer logic is inferred from prose. Per-card mapping remains a review decision.

Latest interface/learning decisions: Place requires both accessible mirror colors. Spell learning is a single icon action, pays exact Element types and equips the first free unlocked slot automatically; plain transfers cannot take Spells. Automatic payment uses local tokens before carried tokens of the same type (implementation default; physical tokens of one Element are equivalent). Required Tool access and Knowledge limits remain enforced. No payment/slot selection form remains below card inspection.

Confirmed Kaleidoscope update: only the very first fragment may start on an outer edge without a neighbor. Every subsequent fragment must share a full edge with an existing fragment, including when selecting another outer-edge cell. This supersedes the older edge-OR-adjacent rule. Existing saved fragments are retained; all future placement follows the new rule. Placement legality is independent of Courage scoring.

Confirmed cumulative adjacency: every existing fragment sharing an edge with the new piece contributes +1 Courage for the same color or +2 for a different color. Add the base +1 once. Repeated colors each count; nonadjacent fragments do not. This supersedes the former provisional single-neighbor/no-stacking interpretation. The Courage track still caps at 35 and eliminated heroes receive no Courage.

Confirmed arrival update: whenever Place brings Occuldor onto a new Black Mirror, every living hero physically in that room loses Courage according to Rage: 0-6 loses 1, 7-13 loses 2, 14-20 loses 3, 21-27 loses 4, and 28 or higher loses 5. All Sigil colors use the same tiers. The designer's "32 to 27" is interpreted as 21-27; the final tier covers the requested range through 38, while normal play ends at Rage 35. This supersedes both the color-specific matrix and the temporary flat loss of 1. Resumed saves use these tiers for future arrivals without retroactive Courage changes. Other rooms are unaffected; arrival does not increase Rage and Collide retains its separate printed penalty.

## Spell activation and interface revision (2026-11-07)

Spell defaults: exclusive alternative modes, one Quick use per Spell per owner turn, and discard temporary assignments on owner death. These are isolated in spellPolicies. Unsupported effects and obsolete hidden-mirror/Companion terminology are listed in SPELL_ACTIVATION_IMPLEMENTATION.md. Legacy completed gem closers are unknown and remain neutral; new placements record ownership.


## Spell catalog v0.4 (2026-11-08)

Designer confirmed obsolete Tool requirements stay blocked and discard means leave in the current room. Portal token count is still unconfirmed (10 configurable provisional). Mode exclusivity, one Quick per turn, additional mandatory movement, relocation connectivity and first-printed-Sigil automatic refresh retain documented defaults.
